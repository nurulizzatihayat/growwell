# Growth curve sources

`growth_curves.csv` contains WHO monthly z-score curve values for boys and girls. The source tables and references are:

- 2006 standards, months 0–60: [length/height](https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards/length-height-for-age), [weight](https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards/weight-for-age), and [BMI](https://www.who.int/toolkits/child-growth-standards/standards/body-mass-index-for-age-bmi-for-age). For height and BMI, the tables use the 0–23 month and 24–60 month series respectively.
- 2007 reference, months 61–228 for height and BMI, and 61–120 for weight: [WHO reference indicators](https://www.who.int/tools/growth-reference-data-for-5to19-years/indicators).

The chart uses the published monthly curve values and plots the unadjusted recorded measurement at completed months. It does not calculate individual z-scores or diagnose. Measurement position is not collected by the form and this may affect younger children's height and BMI comparisons; clinical validation remains necessary.
